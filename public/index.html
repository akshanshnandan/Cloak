Cloak brings your perspective to the actual Apple Store.

  1. 01

    Start with what matters.

    Choose a profile or describe your interests in your own words.

  2. 02

    Let the page come into focus.

    A local, open-weight MiniLM model compares the store’s product descriptions with your interests. Relevant cards get a subtle outline. Apple’s navigation, cards and shopping controls stay in place.

  3. 03

    Make it more you.

    Use More or Less to teach Cloak. Switch to Original whenever you want to restore the page.

First use downloads model weights from Hugging Face. Browsing still connects to Apple. Personal preferences are stored locally. This independent prototype is not affiliated with Apple.
